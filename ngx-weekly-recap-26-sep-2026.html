WEEKLY RECAP · WEEK ENDED 26 SEP 2026

ASI up 0.9% as market cap hits a record ₦163tn

In short

The NGX All-Share Index closed the week at 252,113.41, up 0.9% from 249,804.56 a week earlier. Market capitalisation touched a record ₦163.06 trillion mid-week as bank stocks extended their rally following the CBN’s 350bp rate cut.

Market summary

  • ASI: 252,113.41 on 25 Sep, up 0.92% on the week (from 249,804.56 on 18 Sep).
  • Market cap: record ₦163.06tn on 23 Sep, with the ASI gaining 0.23% in that session.
  • Driver: banking stocks — tier-1 and tier-2 lenders drove volume and value, lifting the Banking Index from 2,658.95 to 2,725.39 points in a single session (22 Sep).
  • Backdrop: the CBN cut the policy rate by 350bps to 23% on 22 Sep; T-bill yields fell the next day and money rotated into equities.
  • Drag: the Dangote Refinery IPO continued to drain liquidity — investors set aside an estimated ₦3.55tn in earlier sessions to fund subscriptions.
